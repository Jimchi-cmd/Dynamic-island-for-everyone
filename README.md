# Islet promo videos

Five short videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, in two versions:

- `islet-<name>-silent.mp4`: no sound. Post these, then add "Everybody Falls" from the app's sound library.
- `islet-<name>.mp4`: with the song, to check the timing.

To download one, click its name above, then click the download button (the arrow) at the top right of the file.

## YouTube tutorial

`islet-youtube-tutorial.mp4` (3 min 10 s) shows how to install Islet on an Android phone, Windows, Mac and Linux, step by step. When you upload it to YouTube, paste the lines from `islet-youtube-tutorial-chapters.txt` into the description: YouTube turns them into chapters.
