# Code of conduct

Islet is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project) is expected to:

- be kind and patient, and assume good intent;
- welcome newcomers and questions of every level;
- critique work, not people, and accept critique of your own work gracefully;
- respect that maintainers and contributors are volunteers with limited time.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks; discriminatory language or imagery; sexual content or attention; publishing someone's private information; sustained disruption; or encouraging any of these.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and warn, suspend or ban participants who break these rules. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository's GitHub profile, or through GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Reports are kept confidential.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
